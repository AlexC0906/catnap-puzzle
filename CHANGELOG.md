# Changelog

## 3.0 — Online and polished

- **Online leaderboard** (Supabase): daily puzzle, all 60 Journey levels and a weekly Marathon board. Submissions are validated by the database; only solves without hints count.
- **Pet the mascot**: it purrs, blushes, floats hearts, meows, shares true cat facts and reacts to the game.
- **Animated walkthrough** in *How to play*.
- **Tap / double-tap controls**: one tap toggles ×, a double-tap places a cat.
- Boards deal in with a wave; placed cats breathe softly.
- **Browser test suite**: 41 Playwright tests on desktop and an emulated phone, run on every push next to the logic tests.
- Phone layout: the game buttons sit right under the board and the mode bar takes one row less.

## 2.5 — More to do

- **Marathon**: as many puzzles as possible in 5 minutes.
- **Share image**: a 1080×1350 card of your solved board.
- Optional procedural **lo-fi music**; cushions glow and chime when they get their cat.

## 2.0 — A complete game

- **Human-style logic solver**: every puzzle is solvable without guessing and rated Easy / Medium / Hard / Expert by the techniques it needs.
- **Explained hints** and a coach mode.
- Modes: **Daily** (with archive and streaks), **Journey** (60 levels in 6 chapters, stars), **Free play**, **Workshop** (design and share your own puzzles).
- 8 unlockable cats, 24 achievements, statistics, settings, mascot, sounds.
- Installable and playable offline (PWA).

## 1.0 — First version

- 5×5 to 10×10 boards with exactly one solution, timer, undo, hints, English / Romanian.
